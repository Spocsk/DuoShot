# DuoShot Studio direction

Scope: landing, workbench, pricing, account, authentication and client review. Visitors are independent iOS developers preparing paired iPhone Duo screenshots; Studio adds client review.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The paired closed/open captures are the product. The landing is a bright product presentation that leads into a quiet working studio.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool white canvas, graphite devices, Geist typography, sea-tinted focus and Harbor imagery. Rounded controls are restrained; color belongs mostly to the screenshots.

**STORY:** See both screen states, understand the checks, enter the atelier, then inspect the exact files before manual App Store Connect upload.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Centered two-line claim and one primary CTA above a large closed/open Harbor pair. Both devices are visible on mobile. The pair occupies the lower half of the desktop opening and carries the visual weight.

**FORM:** User-pinned studio-luminous direction; concept seed 0271bcb2 was run before implementation, and its assignment yields to the user's chosen visual direction. Signature interaction: GSAP moves the pair during the scroll story while the working interface remains fast.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
