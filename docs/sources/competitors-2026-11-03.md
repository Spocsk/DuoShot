## AppScreens — https://appscreens.com/ , https://appscreens.com/pricing
- Free plan $0: essential iOS & Android sizes, up to 5 screens per project, 1 saved project, "Preview Pro features with watermarks", no card required. Free plan: localizations 0 (default language), App Store Connect = manual export.
- Pro: $8.25/month billed annually at $99.00 (tax at checkout). All iOS & Android sizes, translate & localize into 80+ languages, direct upload to App Store & Google Play, 50 saved projects.
- Scale: $15.00/month billed annually at $180.00; extended licence, unlimited apps/projects, up to 25 screens per project.
- Apple sizes list on pricing page includes "iPhone Duo*" where * = subscription required.
- Subscriptions monthly/annual; no one-time plan.

## AppLaunchpad — https://theapplaunchpad.com/
- Browser-based app screenshot generator for App Store & Google Play; "1000+ pre-built templates"; localization to different languages and regions; automatically scales screenshots in all required sizes; FAQ mentions iPhone 6.9", 6.5", iPad 12.9", 13".
- Pricing: not fetched (pricing page returned an error) -> omit price. Watermark: not stated -> omit. Duo: not mentioned on homepage -> "non mentionné sur leur page d'accueil".

## Screenshots.pro — https://screenshots.pro/ , https://screenshots.pro/pricing
- Basic $0 forever: all devices, all pre-made templates, all editor features, smart-export for all sizes. No account needed to create.
- Standard $19/month (adds 3D angles, template storage, custom fonts, localization, standard license). Extended $49/month (API access, extended license). Annual billing "save 35%".
- Localization: automatic translation to any language supported by App Store and Google Play (localization is in Standard plan).
- "23 pixel-perfect devices" e.g. iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPad M4. iPhone Duo not mentioned.
- Watermark: not stated -> omit.
